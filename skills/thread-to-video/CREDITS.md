# Sources and licenses

The repo contains only the short video memes in `assets/memes/` (see below); all other media is not in the repo. `t2v.py setup` (or `t2v.py assets`) downloads them into `~/.thread-to-video/` from the sources listed in `manifest.json`.

- **Music** `assets/music/*` and **background video** `assets/bg/view-of-the-rail-tracks-from-the-train.mp4`: Mixkit (https://mixkit.co), Mixkit License - free for commercial and non-commercial use, no attribution required. Re-check the terms before publishing: https://mixkit.co/license/
- **Gameplay background** `assets/bg/minecraft-parkour.mp4`: "Minecraft Parkour Gameplay NO COPYRIGHT (Vertical)" by the channel *Orbital - No Copyright Gameplay*, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s600FYgI5-s, licensed **CC BY** on YouTube → **credit it when you publish**, e.g. "Gameplay: Orbital - No Copyright Gameplay (CC BY)".
- **Cat memes** `memes/cat-*.webp`: from https://theselfishmeme.co.uk/anh-meme-meo. The site is an aggregator and **states no license**; many source images belong to third parties (some carry other sites' watermarks). Use at your own risk. The images are opaque (no transparency), so they appear as rectangles.
- **Sound effects** `sounds/*.mp3`: from https://www.myinstants.com (user-uploaded, **no stated license**; many are clips from films, memes or songs owned by others). Use at your own risk.
- **Video memes** `memes/*.mp4` (stored in this repo under `assets/memes/`): short reaction clips collected by the repo owner from the web (films, TV, YouTube, Vietnamese internet memes). Original authors are unknown or not tracked and **no license is stated**; they are redistributed here for convenience only. Use at your own risk; if you are a rights holder and want a clip removed, open an issue.
